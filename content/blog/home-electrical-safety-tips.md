Most electrical accidents can be prevented with a few simple habits. Here are six that every home and shop owner should follow.

## 1. Do not overload sockets

Plugging many appliances into one extension board can overheat the cable. Keep heavy appliances like irons, heaters and air conditioners on their own sockets.

## 2. Never replace a breaker with a bigger one

If a breaker keeps tripping, it is warning you about a problem. Fitting a higher-rated breaker only removes the protection and puts the cables at risk. Call an electrician to find the cause.

## 3. Replace damaged cables and sockets

Frayed cables, cracked sockets and loose plugs are dangerous. Do not tape over serious damage and keep using it. Replace the part.

## 4. Keep water away from electricity

Never touch switches or appliances with wet hands. In bathrooms and kitchens, make sure sockets and wiring are properly protected and installed by a qualified person.

## 5. Watch for warning signs

Take these seriously and act straight away:

- A burning smell near a socket or breaker
- Sockets or plugs that feel hot
- Flickering lights that do not improve
- Sparks or buzzing sounds

If you notice any of these, switch off at the main breaker if it is safe to do so and call an electrician.

## 6. Use a qualified electrician

Wiring is not a place to save money. Poor work may look fine for months and then fail when it matters most. A qualified electrician uses the right materials, tests the work and explains what was done.
