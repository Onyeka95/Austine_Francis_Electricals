Old wiring is one of the most common causes of tripping breakers, flickering lights and electrical fires in Nigerian homes. This write-up shows how we approach a full rewiring job so you know what to expect when you hire us.

## The brief

The owner wanted every room rewired, a new distribution board, and enough sockets for modern appliances. The old cables were worn, and the single breaker kept tripping whenever two appliances ran together.

## What we did

- Visited the site, measured every room and listed the sockets, switches and lights needed
- Gave a written quotation with materials and labour shown separately
- Fixed conduit along the walls and ceilings before the cables went in
- Used correctly sized copper cable for lighting, sockets and heavy loads like air conditioners
- Installed a new distribution board with labelled breakers for each circuit
- Tested every circuit before handing over

## The result

The house now has separate circuits for lighting, sockets and heavy appliances, so one fault no longer cuts power to the whole building. Every breaker is labelled, which makes future repairs quick and safe.
